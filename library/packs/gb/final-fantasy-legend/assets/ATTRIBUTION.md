# Asset attribution

- `schematics/overworld.svg` is an original diagram by the Counselor Library
  contributors. It is not a tile render, screenshot, or traced map.
- The Terran map sheets referenced by `sources/reading-plan.md` are local-only
  source material. They are not included in this pack or the repository.
- No sprites, manual scans, screenshots, or game art are included.

The schematic's "unreached" labels are deliberate: they mark what was not walked,
not what does not exist.
