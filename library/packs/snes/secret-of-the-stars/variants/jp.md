# Japanese release

The Japanese release is **Aqutallion** (アクタリオン), a 1,048,576-byte LoROM
image with SHA-1 `cb95d1bfd57e0a226fc7322a572032a6d4a149bf`.

The local JP guide is a 17-page Shift_JIS site with:

- town data: shop stock for Likado, Winds, Beegees, Decatas, and others
- dungeon data: encounter lists and item lists per dungeon
- weapon, item, armor, and non-sale item tables
- magic and magic-learning levels for Aqutallion and Kustera
- a flowchart and a five-part play diary
- small tips

Those pages are local-only. This pack does not reproduce them; the differences
table records the facts that are directly comparable.

## What is safe to say

- The JP title, ROM size, region byte, and character set differ from the USA
  release.
- The shop names in the JP guide use Japanese item names; the USA shop table uses
  short English spellings.
- The JP guide has Kustera-specific magic-learning data, which is useful for the
  colony layer.

## What is open

- Whether the RAM addresses in the local notes are identical on the JP ROM.
- Whether the Aqutallion party slot order maps one-to-one to the USA names.
- Whether any stats, encounter packs, or route gates changed in localization.
- Whether the JP-only diary describes mechanics or just a play route.

The JP guide is a strong source for regional differences, but it is not a
substitute for a ROM comparison.
