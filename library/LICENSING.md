region, and language of sources consulted. The metadata is CC BY-SA 4.0. It does
not relicense the source itself.

## No third-party text

data dumps, or ripped art. Facts are used with provenance; prose is original.
If a source is local-only, its URL, path, and hash stay in `manifest.local.json`,
which is gitignored.
