# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
space where someone represents the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about technical problems and generous with the people solving them.
- Assume the other person is acting in good faith until the record says otherwise.
- Credit sources, testers, and prior work.
- Respect rights holders. A takedown request is not a personal attack.
- Keep criticism about the work: the claim, the evidence, the test, the code.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, slurs, or sustained personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Posting copyrighted guides, ROMs, saves, manuals, or ripped art, or pressuring
  others to do so.
- Using issues to distribute or request pirated material.
- Retaliating against someone who reports a problem.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub's private
reporting or a direct message. Do not open a public issue with personal details
about the people involved. Include what happened, where, and what outcome you
are asking for.

The maintainer will acknowledge the report, avoid conflicts of interest, and
choose a response proportionate to the behavior: a private warning, a request to
edit or remove content, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban. Reports and
identities are kept confidential as far as the investigation allows.
